### 🌸 3-Step Installation Guide:

1. **Download the installer**: click the button above or download [`CariberrySetup.exe`](https://github.com/itz-ranu/Cariberry/releases/latest/download/CariberrySetup.exe).
2. **Run it**: double-click `CariberrySetup.exe` in your Downloads folder. It installs for your account only, with no questions and no administrator prompt.
3. **Meet her**: Cariberry opens by itself and she appears at the bottom of your screen, above the taskbar. Pick your bestie in the panel that opens! 🐾

---

### 💡 First-Time Tips:
* **"Windows protected your PC"**: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show this once. Click **More info ➔ Run anyway**.
* **Her tray icon** (bottom right, by the clock) shows her face. **Click it** for the control panel, or **right-click it** for quick actions. If you can't see it, click the **^** arrow and drag it onto the taskbar.
* **Right-click her** on the desktop to open the same panel next to her.
* She **starts with Windows** from now on. Turn that off in **Settings ➔ Her ➔ Start with Windows**.
* To uninstall: **Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Cariberry**. Her save and settings stay in `%APPDATA%\Cariberry` in case you come back.

---

### 🔒 Privacy Guarantee:
* No telemetry and no trackers. Your focus stats, pet progress and settings are saved only on your PC.
* She reads the title and address of your browser tab to tell Reels from homework. That is matched in memory against your rules and never saved or sent anywhere.
* Chat works with no setup and no internet. Only if **you** connect a chat brain (ranveer sanghvi (ranu), ChatGPT, Gemini or Ollama) are your chat messages sent to it, and nothing else is. Your API key is encrypted for your Windows account.
