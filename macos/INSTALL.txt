# Cariberry for Mac

1. Open Cariberry.dmg.
2. Drag Cariberry onto the Applications folder.
3. Open Cariberry from Applications. She starts with your Mac from then on (turn it off in Settings > Launch at login).

First time on a Mac that is not the one it was built on: if macOS says it cannot verify the app, right-click Cariberry, choose Open, then Open again.
